PROVENANCE — Symfony 8 Preparation, corpus foundation

This project combines the HTTP pilot with an explicitly authorized corpus
import from Sf-8.0 and Symfony-8-Certification-Path. Full original bodies and
original source evidence are retained in a private, non-publishable snapshot.
Only section-level admitted material and reviewed English adapters are eligible
for publication. See README.md for integration status and the build gates.
